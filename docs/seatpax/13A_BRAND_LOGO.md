# Seatpax — Brand & Logo Direction

> Status: **DESIGN BRIEF / READY FOR EXPLORATION**

## Brand idea

Seatpax is a modern bus ticketing and passenger-operations platform. Its logo should not look like a generic bus company logo or a tourism marketplace.

The strongest conceptual ingredients are:

- **seat** — the core inventory object;
- **route / movement** — multi-stop travel and continuity;
- **ticket/pass** — access and validation;
- optionally an abstract **S** for Seatpax.

## Recommended logo direction

Use a **minimal geometric symbol + wordmark**.

Preferred concept:

> An abstract `S` built from two seat-like geometric forms connected by a route/path, subtly suggesting a ticket/pass without literally drawing a bus.

The mark should work at very small sizes as:
- website favicon;
- PWA/app icon;
- QR ticket header;
- admin sidebar mark;
- crew scanner header.

## Avoid

- literal front-view bus illustrations;
- steering wheel icons;
- location-pin clichés as the entire logo;
- airplane-like travel marks;
- overly detailed road illustrations;
- 3D/mockup presentation inside the logo artwork;
- gradients that fail at small icon sizes;
- mascot/cartoon style;
- tiny text inside the symbol.

## Palette direction

Preferred:
- deep navy / dark blue base;
- vivid blue or cyan accent;
- white/light background version;
- one-color version must still work.

## Typography direction

Use a clean modern geometric/grotesk sans-serif wordmark. The word `Seatpax` should feel trustworthy and operational, not luxury-fashion or playful-kids.

## Primary image-generation prompt

```text
Design a professional vector logo identity for “Seatpax”, a modern multi-operator intercity bus ticketing and passenger-operations platform. Create a minimal geometric symbol plus clean wordmark. The symbol should intelligently combine the ideas of a passenger seat, a multi-stop route/path, and a ticket/pass, with a subtle abstract letter “S” if it can be achieved naturally. Do not use a literal bus illustration. The mark must feel fast, reliable, technological, operational, and trustworthy rather than playful or tourism-oriented.

Use simple bold geometry, balanced negative space, strong silhouette, and excellent legibility at small sizes. It must work as a mobile/PWA app icon, favicon, QR-ticket header mark, admin dashboard logo, and crew scanner icon. Prefer a deep navy or dark blue foundation with a vivid blue/cyan accent, plus a fully monochrome version. Flat vector design only: no 3D, no photorealism, no mockup scene, no shadows, no glossy effects, no excessive gradients.

Create a refined custom wordmark reading exactly “Seatpax” beside the symbol using a modern geometric/grotesk sans-serif feel. Keep the typography clean and highly readable. The final identity should visually sit between a modern mobility-tech product and an operational SaaS platform.

Present several coherent logo explorations on a clean neutral background: primary horizontal lockup, standalone symbol/app icon, monochrome version, and small-size favicon test. Keep all concepts from the same design language rather than unrelated logo styles.
```

## Alternative prompt — symbol exploration only

```text
Explore 6 minimal vector symbol concepts for Seatpax, a bus ticketing and passenger-operations technology platform. Focus only on the icon, without a wordmark. Combine seat geometry, multi-stop route movement, ticket/pass structure, and optionally an abstract “S”. Avoid literal buses, generic map pins, steering wheels, wings, and tourist imagery. Use bold simple geometry and clever negative space. The icon must remain recognizable at 16–32 px. Flat two-color system using deep navy and vivid cyan/blue, with every concept also viable in one color. White neutral background, clean vector logo presentation, no 3D mockups.
```

## Evaluation checklist

Choose the concept that best passes these tests:

1. Recognizable at 24px.
2. Still works in one color.
3. Does not depend on the wordmark to be identifiable.
4. Does not look like an airline, travel agency, or generic bus PO.
5. Can sit naturally inside the Seatpax UI design system.
6. Suggests seat + movement/route without becoming illustrative.
7. Looks credible on both consumer ticket screens and B2B admin software.
